# Third-party notices

## Tokenizer vocabulary

`index.html` embeds the first 24,576 merge ranks of the `cl100k_base` tokenizer. They are taken from [js-tiktoken](https://github.com/dqbd/tiktoken) 1.0.15 (MIT License), whose data comes from [openai/tiktoken](https://github.com/openai/tiktoken) (MIT License).

## The paper

The scores, speeds and hyperparameters the page charts and quotes come from B. Minixhofer, T. Murray, T. Limisiewicz, A. Korhonen, L. Zettlemoyer, N. A. Smith, E. M. Ponti, L. Soldaini & V. Hofmann, [Retrofitting language models to operate over bytes](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-026-11111-4), *Nature* (2026), published under CC BY 4.0. Values the paper gives only as plotted points were read off its figures and are marked as such in the page.
